6. Reality check

Can this be automated further?

Yes. The current version is intentionally simple and usable right away.

What is already possible

  • Target small businesses by fit instead of chasing brands that already have full teams.
  • Score leads based on niche, urgency, weak visual presence, and service match.
  • Generate personalized first-touch messaging and call talking points.

Next layer of automation

  • Pull candidate companies from Google Maps, LinkedIn, Instagram, or industry directories.
  • Enrich websites and social profiles to detect weak visuals, missing video, or launch activity.
  • Generate portfolio-aware messages using your best matching case studies.

Later studio-grade version

  • Full CRM with statuses, reminders, notes, and proposal tracking.
  • Automated follow-up sequences and campaign analytics.
  • Client-fit scoring tuned with real reply and conversion data.
