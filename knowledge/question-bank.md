# Candidate Discovery Question Bank

Use these questions to continuously enrich your profile and improve personalization.
Answer in detail; then summarize key facts into `knowledge/master-profile.md`.

## A) Impact and Metrics

1. What is your single most impactful project? Include before/after metrics.
2. What was the hardest production bottleneck you removed?
3. Which output quality metric improved because of your work?
4. What achievement are you most proud of in the last 2 years?

## B) Technical Depth

1. Which tools are you strongest in, and at what level?
2. What technical problem do people usually ask you to solve?
3. Which pipeline tasks can you automate reliably today?
4. Which areas do you want to deepen next (e.g., Unreal shaders, Python tooling)?

## C) Leadership and Collaboration

1. Tell me about a time you influenced teammates without authority.
2. Have you mentored juniors? What changed because of your guidance?
3. Describe a cross-functional conflict and how you resolved it.
4. What is your style under deadline pressure?

## D) Career Intent

1. Which role do you want next: Lead IC, Tech Artist specialist, or people manager?
2. Which industries are preferred (gaming, VFX, retail tech, AR/VR)?
3. What role characteristics are non-negotiable?
4. What type of company environment helps you do your best work?

## E) Cover Letter Signal Enhancers

1. Why this company specifically (not generic)?
2. Which part of the JD maps best to your proven work?
3. What business/user outcome can your experience improve quickly?
4. What 30-60-90 day impact would you aim for?

## F) Objection Handling

1. What concerns might recruiter have (domain fit, salary, notice period)?
2. What evidence can address each concern?
3. Which tradeoffs are you open to?
4. Where should you say "no" clearly?
